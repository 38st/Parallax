# Code of conduct

Parallax is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free
community for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and patient.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect differing experiences and viewpoints.
- Accept feedback gracefully and help keep discussions focused.
- Protect other people's privacy and sensitive information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized language or imagery, personal
attacks, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not
acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in Parallax project spaces and when representing the
project elsewhere. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate
it and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Use GitHub's reporting tools for abusive content or behavior on GitHub. For a
project-specific concern that should be handled privately, open a minimal issue
asking the maintainers for a private contact channel. Do not include sensitive
details in that issue.

Maintainers will consider the context and act in good faith to protect the
community. Retaliation against someone who reports a concern is not acceptable.
